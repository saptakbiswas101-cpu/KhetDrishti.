PRELIMINARY AI-STYLE PREDICTION

Possible causes

Demo estimate based on symptom rules, not a trained AI model or confirmed diagnosis. Do not use it alone to choose a pesticide.

What to do next

Take clear photos of both sides of affected leaves and the whole plant. Check nearby plants. Avoid applying pesticides based only on this estimate. Ask your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture extension officer to confirm the cause, especially if symptoms are spreading.

Your report stays on this device until you choose to share it. Sharing opens your phone's share options; this demo does not send it automatically to a KVK.

FIELD KNOW-HOW

Learn & grow

Simple habits that make crop observations more useful.

Crop observation

How to take a useful crop photo

Capture the whole plant, a close-up of the affected area and both sides of the leaf. Use natural light and keep the camera steady.

Field monitoring

Spot changes early

Walk through different parts of the field regularly. Note when symptoms begin and whether they are spreading.

Safer decisions

Before using a crop treatment

Confirm the problem first. Follow official label directions and seek advice from a qualified local agricultural professional.
